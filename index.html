ABOUT JUMINGXUAN

关于聚茗轩

About Us

聚茗轩茶业专注于中国茶的推广与分享，以茶为媒，连接传统与现代，也连接中国茶与世界。我们甄选具有地域特色与文化底蕴的茶品，希望让更多人从一杯茶开始，认识中国茶的风味与文化。

Jumingxuan Tea is dedicated to sharing the character and culture of Chinese tea. Through carefully selected regional teas, we connect tradition with modern life, and Chinese tea with the world.

TEA CULTURE

茶文化

Tea Culture

一杯茶，不只是味道，也是一种生活方式。温杯、投茶、注水、出汤，每一个简单的动作，都让人与茶、人与人之间多一份从容。

Tea is more than a taste; it is a way of living. From warming the cup to pouring and sharing, every simple movement creates a quieter, more meaningful moment.
